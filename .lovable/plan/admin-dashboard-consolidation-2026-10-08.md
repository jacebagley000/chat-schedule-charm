# Admin dashboard consolidation

## Build
- Make `/admin` the single overview for every business the signed-in account can access.
- Add a visible business list with selection, timezone, and direct calendar/team links.
- Show the selected business’s live appointment totals, upcoming appointment details, and staff roster in the same view.
- Keep the existing plan and upgrade section, service summary, loading, empty, and error states.
- Display appointment times in the selected business’s timezone and refresh appointments and staff when live data changes.

## Validation
- Confirm the page builds cleanly.
- Open `/admin` as the business account and verify businesses, staff, and appointments render without permission or runtime errors.
